# Change handover

## What changed and why

State the concrete problem and resulting behavior in a few sentences.

## Decisions worth knowing

Explain the material choices, trade-offs, and deviations. Flag missing
rationale instead of inventing it. Omit empty sections.

## Review guide

Name the few files where reviewer judgment matters and what to inspect.
Link only material the intended reviewer can access.

## Verification and known gaps

Record actual checks, their results and state, untested behavior, and remaining
assumptions. A prepared handover does not imply the change was published.
